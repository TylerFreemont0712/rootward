# Reverse the Scroll

A scroll in the Guild archive was copied backwards, one word at a time.

For each line of input, print its words in reverse order, separated by single spaces. Words are separated by any
amount of whitespace, and punctuation stays attached to its word. A line with no words prints an empty line.

```
input:  hello brave new world
          spaced   out
output: world new brave hello
        out spaced
```

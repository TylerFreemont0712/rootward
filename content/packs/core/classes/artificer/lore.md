# Artificer

The Guild's oldest discipline. Artificers believe every problem is a function waiting to be named. They carry a
Spellbook, which is a notebook of things they got right once and refuse to write again. Their weakness is
elegance: an Artificer will polish a solution while the Goblin eats their Focus.

**Voice.** Precise, a little vain about naming, allergic to magic numbers.

**Fights in** the editor. **At home in** the Foundry, the Grove, and the Spire. **Uneasy in** the Kernel Halls,
where nothing has a return value and everything has a side effect.
